Who i am

Right now, I am focused on the UX/UI Designer role with +6 years of experience (since 2019), adopting a strategic approach to end-to-end digital products.

My background spans various industries with complex projects — primarily in banking — alongside experience in logistics, and retail products, designing solutions that balance user experience and needs with business goals.

Degree in Advertising Art Direction with over 12 years of experience in the agency and creative studio ecosystem.

Scale is the true challenge of product design.

As a product grows, a lack of visual and technical cohesion can emerge, creating friction in the user experience that ultimately fragments usability.

My work focuses on connecting product decisions through solid flows—turning friction into efficient experiences and consistent products.

Efficiency for Designers & Developers.

With technical documentation detailing behaviors, tokens, and spacing, we guarantee an efficient handoff. We don't just design screens—we create a clear system for development, product, and other stakeholders. This reduces friction in the codebase by aligning Figma with Storybook, ensuring both designers and developers understand the true scope and capabilities of every component.

Accessibility Assurance (A11Y).

Accessibility is non-negotiable. It is documented to ensure every component complies with WCAG standards—defining contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, and specific behaviors so the product remains inclusive and compliant with current regulations.

Screen Analysis.

Analyzing each screen not in isolation, but within its specific use case. This allows us to spot inconsistencies, reuse components, and align behaviors with the needs of the flow.

Visual Consistency.

Typography, color palettes, spacing, layouts, and grids—ensuring the interface feels cohesive, even if the user can't explicitly point out why.

Functional Consistency.

If two elements look similar, they should behave the same way. When they don't, it creates doubt. And when users hesitate, the experience breaks down.
